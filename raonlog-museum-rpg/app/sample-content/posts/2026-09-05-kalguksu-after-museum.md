There is a kalguksu place two streets from Ichon station that we have now been to three times, always in the rain. The broth is anchovy and something else the owner will not tell us.

Raon eats the noodles first and leaves the clams for Dad, which Dad considers a fair trade. This time he also ate the kimchi, which is new.

We paid, put our hoods up, and walked back to the station arguing about whether the museum or the noodles were the best part. The noodles won, two to one.
