A mat, kimbap, and the first time Raon rode a bicycle without the little wheels. It happened on the grass by Ichon Hangang Park at about four in the afternoon, after two falls and one dramatic lie-down.

Shun ran alongside for the first ten meters and then he was gone, wobbling toward the river with the confidence of someone who has not yet learned about brakes.

We had pizza afterward, because that is what you do after a first bicycle ride.
