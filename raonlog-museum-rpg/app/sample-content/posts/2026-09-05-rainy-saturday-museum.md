It rained the way Seoul rains in September, sideways. We took Line 4 to Ichon and walked the covered path to the museum. Raon counted seventeen umbrellas before we reached the door.

Inside, the Path to History was quieter than we expected. We went straight up to the Room of Quiet Contemplation while it was still empty and stood there for about four minutes, which for a seven-year-old is a very long time. He asked why the Buddha was half-smiling. We said nobody has told him the answer yet either.

Then we came down and found the celadon. Raon asked whether the fish on the vase were real. We said yes. He accepted this, and we left for kalguksu at 12:40, wet and pleased.

Tips for families: go on a Wednesday or Saturday evening if you can, when it is open late and quieter. Start with the Room of Quiet Contemplation before the queue forms. The children's museum needs a reservation.
