We brought home a small cat plushie from the museum shop. Messi, our actual cat, regarded it for a full minute from the top of the bookshelf, then came down, sniffed it twice, and sat on it.

This is, we think, approval. The plushie now lives under the sofa, where Messi keeps things she has decided are hers. Raon wanted it back. Negotiations are ongoing.
